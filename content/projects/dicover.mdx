## What is Discover Jobs Platform?
DiscoverJobs is a modern and responsive job board application built to connect job seekers with relevant opportunities. It features job listings, detailed job descriptions, search and filtering capabilities, and a user-friendly interface. The platform also includes a built-in resume builder with modern, customizable templates, allowing candidates to create professional resumes directly within the site. Designed with simplicity and usability in mind, DiscoverJobs streamlines the job-hunting process by providing a clean and intuitive experience for users.
